## PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

### Before Recording:
- [ ] Script memorized (not reading from paper)
- [ ] Product working smoothly (no glitches)
- [ ] Camera positioned at eye level (6-10 feet away)
- [ ] Lighting tested (professional or natural light)
- [ ] Audio tested (quiet room, external microphone)
- [ ] Multiple backup copies planned

### During Recording:
- [ ] Camera on (rolling before first word)
- [ ] Eye contact with camera (not looking down)
- [ ] Speak naturally (don't sound robotic)
- [ ] Pauses between major points (1-2 seconds)
- [ ] Do multiple takes (pick the best one)
- [ ] Save video immediately after

### After Recording:
- [ ] Voiceover synced with visuals
- [ ] Audio levels consistent throughout
- [ ] No background noise
- [ ] Transitions smooth
- [ ] Timing is ≤3 min (pitch) or ≤2 min (demo)
- [ ] File size <100MB
- [ ] Resolution 1920x1080 minimum

### Before Uploading:
- [ ] Video plays on different devices
- [ ] Audio is clear and professional
- [ ] Quality looks good on projector-sized screen
- [ ] Backup copy saved separately
