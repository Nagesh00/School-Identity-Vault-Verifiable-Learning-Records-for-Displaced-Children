# School Identity Vault - Pitch Video Script (3 Minutes)
## Professional, Compelling Narrative for Imagine Cup

---

## SCRIPT OVERVIEW

**Duration:** Exactly 3 minutes (180 seconds)
**Delivery:** 2-3 speakers (can be one person)
**Setting:** Professional - office, classroom, or clean background
**Camera:** Static, positioned as if judge is facing you
**Tone:** Passionate but professional, authentic, compelling

**Timing Breakdown:**
- Introduction: 0:00-0:30 (30 seconds)
- Problem: 0:30-1:00 (30 seconds)  
- Solution: 1:00-1:45 (45 seconds)
- Validation: 1:45-2:15 (30 seconds)
- Impact & Vision: 2:15-2:50 (35 seconds)
- Call to Action: 2:50-3:00 (10 seconds)

---

## FULL SCRIPT

### [0:00-0:30] INTRODUCTION & HOOK

**Speaker 1** (energetic, confident, eye contact with camera):

---

"Hi, I'm [Name], and this is [Name and Name]. Together, we're the founding team of School Identity Vault.

[PAUSE - 1 second]

We're here to tell you about a problem affecting 258 million children worldwide.

These children have lost the one thing that could change their lives: proof of their education."

---

**Timing:** Read this naturally, not too fast. Emphasize "258 million" and "proof of their education."

**Visuals During This Section:**
- Opening slide with team
- Title slide: School Identity Vault logo
- Stat: "258 Million Displaced Children"
- B-roll: Children studying, diverse classroom scenes

---

### [0:30-1:00] PROBLEM STATEMENT

**Speaker 2** (passionate, personal connection):

---

"Last year, I met Amira and Hassan in Lebanon. They fled Syria with almost nothing.

But they had documentation that their son, Karim, had completed ninth grade. He was a good student.

When they tried to enroll him in school in Lebanon, the school said, 'We can't verify this. We need official documents.'

The family had nothing. His school in Syria was destroyed in the war.

So Karim couldn't be enrolled. Not because he wasn't smart enough. Not because he didn't deserve it.

But because he lost his education records in a war he didn't cause."

---

**Timing:** Tell this as a story, not a statistic. Make eye contact. Pause for emotional weight.

**Key Performance Points:**
- Speak slowly and deliberately
- Make eye contact with camera
- Show genuine emotion (not acting)
- Pause after "he couldn't be enrolled" for impact

**Visuals During This Section:**
- Subtle b-roll: Refugee families, schools, documents
- Stat on-screen: "80% of displaced children lose education records"
- Stat on-screen: "Average 1-2 year learning setback"
- Map showing displacement hotspots

---

### [1:00-1:45] SOLUTION PRESENTATION

**Speaker 1** (confident, demonstrative):

---

"This is where School Identity Vault comes in.

We've built a solution using AI and blockchain that makes education portable, verifiable, and impossible to lose.

Here's how it works in three simple steps:

**STEP 1 - Upload**

Karim's parents take a photo of his old school certificate with their phone. Even if it's blurry or worn. They upload it to our system.

**STEP 2 - AI Processing**

Our system uses Microsoft AI to:
- Read the text from the image (even in Arabic, even blurry)
- Extract the education data (grades, subjects, dates)
- Generate a clean, standardized profile
- Calculate confidence scores so schools know what to trust

All of this happens in less than a minute.

**STEP 3 - Verify & Record**

A school or education authority reviews the profile, approves it, and we record it on blockchain.

Now Karim has a verified credential. Portable. Tamper-proof. Impossible to lose.

If his family has to flee again, Karim has proof of his education. Locked in, forever."

---

**Timing Notes:**
- Speak at natural, confident pace
- Emphasize the three steps (pause briefly between each)
- When mentioning "Microsoft AI" make eye contact - this is important to judges
- Pause after "forever" for impact

**Visuals During This Section:**
- Live demo OR screenshots of actual product showing:
  - Document upload interface
  - Processing in progress (show Microsoft AI logos)
  - Profile creation with confidence scores
  - Verification screen
  - Final credential display
- Or: Animation showing 3-step process
- Graphics overlaid: "Azure Computer Vision" "Azure Language Service" "Azure OpenAI" "Azure Machine Learning"
- Final graphic: Blockchain lock symbol + "Immutable Record"

---

**Timing Notes:**
- Speak clearly and slowly
- Allow time for quotes to be read on-screen
- Use real quotes from your interviews (not invented ones)
- End strong: "The demand is real. The need is urgent. And our solution works."

**Visuals During This Section:**
- Quote cards appearing one-by-one (animated or static)
- Headshots or respectful photos of customers (if available)
- Metrics displayed:
  - "50 pilot users"
  - "96% completion"
  - "9.1/10 satisfaction"

Futures are secured.

**Timing Notes:**
- Build momentum as you speak about impact
- Pause for emotional weight at key moments
- Make eye contact during "Futures are secured"

**Visuals During This Section:**

  - "500 families" (Year 1)

Imagine Cup, we're asking for:

**Timing Notes:**

**Visuals During This Section:**
- All team members visible together (final shot)
- Quote on-screen: "Together, we can secure education for 258 million children"

## SPEAKER NOTES FOR PRACTICE

### Delivery Guidelines:

**Pacing:**
- Don't rush. Judges need time to absorb.
- Aim for natural speaking pace (140-160 words per minute)
- Pause intentionally for impact

**Eye Contact:**
- Look directly at camera (imagine a person is watching)
- Don't read from script - internalize it
- Glance down only if absolutely necessary

**Energy Level:**
- Start confident and energetic
- Match emotional tone to content (personal story = more intimate, vision = passionate)
- Don't be overly theatrical (professional, not Broadway)

**Transitions Between Speakers:**
- If multiple speakers, have smooth handoffs
- Brief eye contact between speakers adds warmth
- Each speaker should have distinct but complementary energy

**Emphasis Points:**
- "258 million" - Let the magnitude sink in
- "Proof of their education" - This is the heart of the problem
- "Microsoft AI" - Judges need to hear this
- "Our solution works" - Confidence here
- "Futures are secured" - Vision/emotional close

### Tone Guidance:

**NOT:** Corporate, scripted, wooden, fake emotion
**YES:** Authentic, passionate, compelling, professional

---

## VISUAL NOTES FOR VIDEO PRODUCTION

### Camera Setup:
- Static camera, positioned at eye level
- Positioned as if camera is a judge/investor sitting across from you
- 6-10 feet away (medium shot, can see upper body and facial expressions)
- Good lighting (professional studio lighting ideal, or good natural light)
- Neutral background (office, classroom, or simple backdrop)

### Audio:
- Professional microphone (lavalier or boom)
- Quiet room with good acoustics
- No background noise (turn off phones, air conditioning)
- Test audio levels before recording

### Editing:
- Minimize editing - keep it natural
- Cut out long pauses or mistakes
- Add intro/outro graphics (School Identity Vault logo)
- Embed product demo video during solution section (if doing demo vs. talking)
- No fancy transitions - keep it professional

### File Requirements:
- Format: MP4 or MOV
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Duration: ≤ 3 minutes (ideally 2:50-3:00)
- File size: < 100MB
- Bitrate: 5-8 Mbps (high quality)

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Reading from script** - Internalize talking points, speak naturally
❌ **Speaking too fast** - Slow down, let impact sink in
❌ **No eye contact** - Look at camera as if looking at judge
❌ **Fake emotion** - Authentic passion is more compelling than acting
❌ **Too much jargon** - Explain Microsoft AI services in simple terms
❌ **No customer validation** - Judges need to hear from real customers
❌ **Unclear problem** - Make sure problem is crystal clear before solution
❌ **Ignoring competition** - Mention what makes you different
❌ **No call to action** - Tell judges what you're asking for
❌ **Too much content** - Focus on core narrative, not all details
