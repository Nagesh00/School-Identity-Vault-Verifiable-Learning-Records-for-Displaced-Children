## SLIDE 1: TITLE SLIDE
### Visual Design Prompt:

**Layout:**
- Full-screen background image
- Text overlay: Center-aligned, bold typography
- Logo placement: Top-right corner

**Background Image:**
- High-quality photo of diverse children in classroom
- OR: Abstract world map with education icons
- Tone: Hopeful, inclusive, professional
- Color treatment: Slight gradient overlay (blue/teal, semi-transparent)

**Text Elements:**
```
Primary Title (Large):
"School Identity Vault"
Font: Bold sans-serif, 72pt+, white
Position: Upper-center

Subtitle:
"Preserving Education. Securing Futures."
Font: Lighter sans-serif, 48pt, white
Position: Center

Team Name:
[Your Team Name]
Font: Regular sans-serif, 32pt, light gray
Position: Lower-left

Institution:
[University/School Name]
Font: Regular sans-serif, 24pt, light gray
Position: Bottom-left
```

**Logo Elements:**
- School Identity Vault logo (if created): Top-right, white/light colored
- Institution logo: Bottom-right, professional
- Imagine Cup badge: Optional, if applying

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep blue (#1E40AF)
- Secondary: Teal (#0891B2)
- Accent: Green (#10B981)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Background: Dark blue-gray gradient

**Design Notes:**
- Professional but warm (not corporate sterile)
- Conveys trust, education, technology, hope
- Immediately signals global/humanitarian mission
- No clutter - clean, memorable first impression
